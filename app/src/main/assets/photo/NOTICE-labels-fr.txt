Blamera French display names — 4585 position-aligned YOLOE categories.

The original ordered English vocabulary is unchanged. Its origin and AGPL-3.0
model notice are described in photo/NOTICE.txt and benchmark/LICENSE-models-AGPL.txt.
The French catalogue is a derived display resource, not a replacement vocabulary
for inference. Distinct original category IDs are retained, including synonyms.

Initial French translations were generated locally on the development PC using:
Helsinki-NLP / OPUS-MT — opus-mt-tc-big-en-fr, release 2022-03-09,
revision 6e062862ced6f5622a589ab2aadc2a1d4978db78.
https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-tc-big-en-fr/tree/6e062862ced6f5622a589ab2aadc2a1d4978db78

Thottingal, “OPUS-MT — Building open translation services for the World”, 2020.

Changes by Blamera: positional conversion to French display strings, NFC
normalization, explicit lexical corrections, French alternatives for ambiguous
isolated source words, and reuse of the project's existing French COCO names.
Translation/correction agents read all pairs and targeted likely lexical errors;
this is not expert linguistic certification. Some proper/scientific names and
French loanwords legitimately match English. Opaque original labels “power see”,
“jackcrab” and truncated “bonasa umbellu” remain explicit documented exceptions.
No endorsement by the model authors is implied. This notice conservatively
preserves CC BY attribution for the preparation model and derived translations;
it does not replace the original vocabulary/model licence.

Only the static French catalogue and provenance are embedded in Blamera.
No translation weights or translation SDK are installed on the phone. No photos
or personal data were used or sent to a translation service. Preparation was
free, offline CPU inference after public model download.

Hashes, generation settings, source alignment and correction references:
photo/labels-fr-provenance.json and experiments/m1-labels-fr/ in Blamera sources.
